        # Spinning

        After sizing, the boiled thread fitted on a danganatha (big size reel) for dried.
The skein of thread wound at a stretch and then removed from the reel. The
skeins of thread then spread on one pair of gonsi (flat long split bamboo) and
let dry in the sunshine. When the threads are dry, these fitted on chwrkhi (small
size reel) for spinning. The chwrkhi placed on the ground and then the threads
are spins around it from the zenther (spinning wheel). Usually, it makes the
thread rounds bigger, which is convenient for weaving and removes the knots
of the thread. Then the threads spun again into smaller spools called the
Bobbin. The zenther runs by single-handed woman who spins the thread into
the wheel with one hand that other hand used for wrapping the thread into the
spools. The Spinning or winding is known as khundung thunnai in Bodo.
