        # Warping

        The warping is the third stage of the weaving techniques after sizing and
spinning. Specially, the process involves transferring yarn from several of
tubes, cones or cheeses positioned on the creel onto a gandwi (warp beam). It
requires numbers of bobbins and then bobbins are placed on a huge wooden
made frame creel. After fitting the bobbins in creel, the ends of the bobbins
passed through the hank frame. The length of the warp is depends on the
circumference of the drum, reed and number of bobbins fitted on creel that the
process is known as Ji Swngnai in Bodo.
