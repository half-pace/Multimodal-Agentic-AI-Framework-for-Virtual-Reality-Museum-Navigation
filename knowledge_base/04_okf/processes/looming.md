        # Looming

        The technique of looming process is consists of drafting, denting and heald
knitting for proper fitting up of warp on the loom that to complete the
necessary arrangements for weaving.  Drafting- This process is known as Nw
khonnai in Bodo. Traditionally, the white strong cotton thread used for drafting.
The beam is places on the floor at the same level against two posts and tightly
fixing it with two temporary bamboo posts. It helps to keep the yarns in parallel
balance that the drafting process completed properly. The Baleb (flat bamboo
sticks) used as lease-rods and keep upward in the warp for causing a slight
opening on it. The drafting is done with the help of Nw hasung (a cylindrical
bamboo pipe) by heald passed from right to left.  Denting- The denting is
known as khainai in Bodo. It is a traditional weaving technique of Bodos. The

warp threads drawn through the dents of the rashw (reed) with the help of a
hook and the yarns placed on a gonshi (a round bamboo stick).  Heald- The
heald is a most important component of a weaving. It is the processing of yarn
in the loom with the help of shedding. The heald an arrangement for made to
tie up the yarn with necessary connections. After completed all the mentioned
process, the warp fitted to the cloth beam then it become ready for weaving.
