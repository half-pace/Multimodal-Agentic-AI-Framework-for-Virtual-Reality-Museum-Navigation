        # Weaving

        The weaving has started after sizing, spinning, warping, denting, beaming and
drafting or heald knitting. Nevertheless, there is some traditional process of
preparing the yarn for the convenient of weaving. Generally, the Bodo women
have folk used “Sal” (throw shuttle loom) and “Thati sal” or “Tat sal”
(Fly-Shuttle Loom) which are the traditional type of loom. These types of
traditional looms are very simple and possess with special features. We have
found that the looms consist of four wooden or bamboo made stout posts.
Sometimes, betel nut tree also used as loom posts. Besides this, all related
weaving implements mostly made of bamboo and wood. Usually, the weaver
sits in front of a loom keeping both legs against the footrest and fixing the back
strap, which is adjustable for keeping the loom in tightness. The shedding
motion, the picking motion and the beating motion govern the weaving. The
heald bar lifted up with the left hand and simultaneously the right hand presses
the circular bamboo bar down. Then the sword placed vertically in the shed and
the weft passed from the right to left side by the right hand that the shuttle
picked up by the left hand. The sword has beaten the weft slowly. The weaving
sword then taken out and the shuttle is passed by the left hand and picked up by
the right hand. At the time of weaving, the two bamboo splits work as the first
weft. This is the traditional weaving technique of Bodos. Mentioned may be
made here, this traditional technique takes 30 to 45 days to completion of
weaving process. We have come to know that 2-3 pairs of cloths need at least 3
days.
