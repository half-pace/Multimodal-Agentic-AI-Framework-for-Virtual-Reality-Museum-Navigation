# Reeling
        
Among the Bodos both rearing and reeling operations are traditional
techniques. The reeling has mostly done on the mulberry cocoons. Firstly, the
cocoons boiled in a vessel with water by fire or softened by steam. Number of
selected cocoons are kept floating on the surface of water. After over the
process the cocoons packet by leaves of tharai (Curcuma rubescens) and
followed by pressed with some heavy weight to removes the water completely.
The cocoons are bring out from packet after 5 to 6 days and washed thoroughly
to make it ready for reeling. After completion of this process, the cocoons
gradually started to reel by the women folk on her left hand by right palm.
