        # Beaming

        The reed is move carefully after denting towards the warp and transferring the
warp sheet into weavers beam. In this process, a round bamboo stick with the
strands of yarn passes through the reeds and sheet of threads is placed in the
grove of beam and wound with a rope that known as phannai in Bodo.
