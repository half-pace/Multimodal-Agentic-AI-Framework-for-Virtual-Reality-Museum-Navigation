# Winding
        
This process is necessary that the yarn tread convert to a suitable form to make
it convenient to prepare the warp. As using of traditional techniques, the
method widely acceptable amongst the Bodo weavers and they are winding of
yarn on bobbins (for warp). The swrkhi (winding drum wheel) can be use to
wind weft yam on pirn.
