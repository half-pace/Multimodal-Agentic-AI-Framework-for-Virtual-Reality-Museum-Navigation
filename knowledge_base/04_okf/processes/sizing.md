# Sizing
        
The sizing is a pre weaving process, which is unique technique of handloom
weaving. Sizing is a coating of lubricate mixture of natural ingredients
containing like – rice powder, maize, wheat flour etc. which is overlap on the
surfaces of the yarn and it makes the yarn strength, rounder in shape and
uniform in diameter. The sizing processing called Maidi hwnai among the
Bodos. During the process of sizing, ingredients are mixes in a big tube and
boiled it with hot water. The process is carries out by the skilled weavers in the

village. The yarns then treated in the hot sizing solution and care has taken to
see that the application of size is satisfactory. The special brushes used to
spread the boiled threads and dry the starch on the yarn. Eventually, depending
on the warp length, three to four persons are required to carry out sizing
process. However, while preparing muga or eri yarn for weaving, the threads
are soaked for 1 to 2 hours in a light warm hot water, but never boiled. For fine
and strong yarn, the traditional sizing process is necessary.
